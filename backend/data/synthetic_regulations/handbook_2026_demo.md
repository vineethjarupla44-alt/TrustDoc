### CHAPTER 1: LAND & LOCATION NORMS

#### Clause 1.1: Minimum Contiguous Land Extent
- **SYNTH-NORM-1.1.1 (Urban / Metro Category)**: An institution offering Undergraduate Degree in Engineering & Technology in Urban/Metro territory must possess a minimum of **2.50 Acres** (or 1.01 Hectares) of contiguous, unencumbered land.
- **SYNTH-NORM-1.1.2 (Rural Category)**: In Rural or non-metro areas, the minimum required contiguous land area is **5.00 Acres**.
- **SYNTH-NORM-1.1.3 (Title & Ownership)**: Land must be owned via a registered Sale Deed or registered perpetual lease (minimum 30 years). Title Deed must contain clear survey numbers, subdivision bounds, and registration date.

### CHAPTER 2: BUILT-UP INFRASTRUCTURE & MASTER PLAN

#### Clause 2.1: Master Plan and Floor Area
- **SYNTH-NORM-2.1.1 (Architectural Endorsement)**: All site plans and building layout diagrams must be certified and signed by an Architect registered with the Council of Architecture (COA). The architect's unique registration number (Format: `CA/YYYY/NNNNN`) and seal are mandatory.
- **SYNTH-NORM-2.1.2 (Sanctioning Authority)**: Master Plan must bear the official sanction seal and approval number of the competent Urban Planning / Town & Country Planning / Municipal Corporation department.
- **SYNTH-NORM-2.1.3 (Instructional Area)**: Minimum carpet area for academic classrooms, lecture halls, and laboratories must not fall below 4,000 square meters for an intake of 300 students.

### CHAPTER 3: LIFE SAFETY & FIRE CLEARANCE

#### Clause 3.1: Fire Safety Certificate (Fire NOC)
- **SYNTH-NORM-3.1.1 (Issuing Authority)**: The Fire Safety Certificate / No-Objection Certificate (NOC) must be issued by the State Fire Services Department or Chief Fire Officer.
- **SYNTH-NORM-3.1.2 (Active Validity)**: The certificate must be currently active and unexpired. If the validity period has lapsed at the date of application evaluation, the application shall be flagged as DEFICIENT.
- **SYNTH-NORM-3.1.3 (Building Height & Access)**: Fire clearance must verify access roads of minimum 9.0 meters width and operative water hydrant systems.

### CHAPTER 4: FACULTY & CADRE RATIO

#### Clause 4.1: Student-Faculty Ratio (SFR)
- **SYNTH-NORM-4.1.1 (Prescribed Ratio)**: For undergraduate Engineering programs, the overall Student-to-Faculty Ratio (SFR) shall not exceed **1:15** (or 1:20 for approved tier-2 programs).
- **SYNTH-NORM-4.1.2 (Cadre Composition)**: The teaching faculty cadre ratio shall maintain a balance of approximately **1 Professor : 2 Associate Professors : 6 Assistant Professors**.
- **SYNTH-NORM-4.1.3 (Principal / Director)**: The institution must have an appointed regular Principal/Director with Ph.D. credentials and approved council seniority.
